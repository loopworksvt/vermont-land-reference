Hunting and trapping seasons, 2026-2027

seasons

Season dates for Vermont's 2026 hunting year in one place. Dates are inclusive. Seasons change every year, and the Fish & Wildlife Board can change them; this page is updated each year from the department's published summary. A license, tags and permits are required; check Vermont Fish & Wildlife for the current regulations, bag limits and any area rules before you hunt or trap. This is a summary, not the regulation.

Species marked here have a page in this library: White-tailed Deer, Black Bear, Moose, Wild Turkey, Snowshoe Hare, Eastern Cottontail, Eastern Gray Squirrel, Ruffed Grouse, American Woodcock, the foxes, coyote, raccoon and other fur-bearers, and the ducks and geese.

Moose (Moose)

Moose are managed by a permit lottery, by management unit. The season dates the department lists, if permits are issued:

Ducks, geese and other migratory birds

Regulations in effect Sept. 1, 2026 through March 10, 2027. Vermont has three waterfowl zones: the Lake Champlain Zone, the Interior Vermont Zone and the Connecticut River Zone.

Youth Waterfowl Hunting Weekend: September 26 and 27, 2026. Hunters 17 and younger may hunt ducks, geese, mergansers and coots in the Lake Champlain and Interior Vermont Zones; in the Connecticut River Zone, youth must be 15 or younger. Each youth must be licensed and accompanied by a licensed adult 18 or older.

Daily bag limits (the possession limit is three times the daily limit, except for snow geese): ducks 6 (18 in possession); mergansers 5; coots 15; brant 1; snow geese 25 with no possession limit. Canada geese: September season 8 in the Lake Champlain and Interior Zones and 5 in the Connecticut River Zone; October and November season 3 in the Lake Champlain and Interior Zones and 2 in the Connecticut River Zone; December and January season 5 in all three zones.

The daily limit of 6 ducks may include no harlequin and no more than 4 mallards (2 of them hens), 2 black ducks (American Black Duck), 3 wood ducks, 3 pintail, 2 canvasbacks, 2 redheads, 2 or 1 scaup depending on dates, and 4 sea ducks.
