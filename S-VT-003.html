File by February 1 a report of forestry activity in the previous calendar year; no form is needed if there was none. Can be filed online or on paper.

What counts and what does not

File a form for each calendar year in which management activity happened on the enrolled land, by February 1 of the next year (an FMAR due February 1, 2025 covers 2024). Reportable activity includes timber harvesting, other vegetation management, sugaring, invasive plant control projects, planting and habitat work. The Standards exempt routine maintenance with no harvest of forest products (clearing blowdown from trails, cleaning culverts and waterbars, boundary painting, general monitoring of invasive plants), personal firewood of 10 cords or less in a year, and sugaring under 200 taps. A county forester's 2026 reminder to an owner stated the rule more broadly (maple sugaring, timber harvesting, other vegetation management; no form for a year with no activity); where the two differ, the Standards' exceptions apply. Failure to file may result in removal from enrollment. The blank form is available online and from any county forester.
