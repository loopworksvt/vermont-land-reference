Use Value Rates (Vermont Current Use, per acre)

ratesRT-VT-001

A summary of published values, not a tax bill. The Department of Taxes' current notice decides; a year marked "not in hand" was not read.

Set annually by the Department of Taxes (tax year begins April 1). The use value of enrolled acres is acres times use value times the common level of appraisal (CLA). Savings per year are (full assessed value of the enrolled land minus its use value) times 0.01 times the tax rate per $100.
