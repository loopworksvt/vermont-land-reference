Names

Redtop, Agrostis gigantea, a bentgrass in the family Poaceae. Go Botany also lists the synonym Agrostis alba (auct. non L.), which older books may use.

Range and habitat

Not native. Go Botany says it is not native to North America, is found throughout New England including Vermont, and grows in disturbed or maintained ground, meadows and fields, roadsides and shores; it is usually found in wetlands (wetland status FACW). The fire-effects page says it occurs from Newfoundland south to northern Georgia and Alabama, in wet to moist meadows, tolerating some flooding and moderately acid soils, and not suited to limey soil. The UVM 2004 chart lists redtop as a perennial pasture grass.

How to identify (by season; look-alikes and the key difference)

  • Growth habit (UVM): one of the bentgrasses: low-growing, fine, soft-leaved grasses often found in low, wet areas of pastures and meadows. They form soft, dense patches. Leaves are rolled in the bud shoot.
  • Which bentgrass: by the UVM table, creeping bentgrass spreads by stolons only, colonial bentgrass by rhizomes and stolons, and redtop by rhizomes only. Ligules differ: creeping long, pointed to round; colonial short, truncate; redtop medium, rounded. Go Botany gives redtop's ligule as 1 to 7 mm and colonial bentgrass's as 0.5 to 2 mm.
  • Leaf: blades 3 to 9 mm wide, flat or slightly folded, rough; no auricles (Go Botany).
  • Seedhead: a spread-out, branched panicle 80 to 300 mm long (Go Botany) with tiny one-flowered spikelets 2 to 3.5 mm long.
  • Height: 20 to 120 cm (Go Botany).
  • Look-alikes: colonial bentgrass (shorter ligule) and creeping bentgrass (stems that lie down at the base, yellow-brown seedhead) (Go Botany).

Role in the field

The fire-effects page says redtop spreads rapidly with strong rhizomes and colonizes disturbed sites, and that reed canarygrass can invade redtop meadows (Reed Canarygrass).

Managing for it and living with it

The sources read give no mowing advice for redtop.

Legal status

Not on the Agency of Agriculture quarantine list as read on 2026-10-30. No other legal status was looked up.
