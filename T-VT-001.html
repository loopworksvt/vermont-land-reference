Cutting contrary to the approved plan

tripwireT-VT-001

A summary, not legal advice. This is what your reference library records about the rule, not a ruling on your land. Who to ask: county forester, current use program office, consulting forester.

Timber harvesting on enrolled land is allowed, but only as the approved plan prescribes. A view opening is not a plan treatment, so cutting trees for a view on enrolled land needs an approved amendment first, or a forester-led harvest confirmed as consistent with the plan. Cutting contrary to the plan "during the remaining term of the plan" is development under the statute.

Before cutting: confirm in writing (a) whether the land is still enrolled on the date you will cut, (b) that the trees are not in a stream or wetland buffer, and (c) who is cutting and whether they follow the accepted management practices. If land has left the program, cutting is no longer a program matter, but state wetland and water quality rules still apply.

What triggers it
Cutting trees: trees are cut that the approved plan does not prescribe, or outside its timing window (3 years either side of a prescribed year), on enrolled land during the plan term
What follows
treated as development: land use change tax on the affected land, removal of the parcel from the program, and a five-year bar on re-enrolling (Standards §2.3.1, §2.3.3)
How to avoid it
get the county forester's approval of a plan amendment BEFORE cutting; or cut only after the land has left the program, confirmed in writing
Set by
county forester (FPR); Department of Taxes
Source
32 V.S.A. § 3752(5)(C); Standards 2026 §2.3.1, §2.3.3

Before contracting a logger

A county forester's 2026 reminder to an owner: call the consulting forester or the county forester before contracting with anyone to log the woodlot. Cutting that the plan does not prescribe is treated as development: the entire parcel comes out of enrollment, the Land Use Change Tax is assessed on the acres subject to the development, and the parcel is ineligible for five years (Standards §2.3.1; 32 V.S.A. chapter 142). The opposite failure also counts: not carrying out prescribed management inside its window can lead to removal.
