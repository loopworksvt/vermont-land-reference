Vermont Public Data Sources

sources

A guide to where to look, not a ruling. Each row says what the source gives and, where known, whether it was read; means it is only named..

What each public source gives, so you know where to look. Interactive tools, with links that open them at a place, are in Vermont Tools and Resources.

SourceGivesNotes
Town listers or assessorgrand list acres, assessed value, land-only value, the Current Use exemption, tax bill, parcel IDland use change tax needs the assessors' separate-parcel value of the changed land

UVA Stewardship Guide for Vermont Forest Landowners (anrmaps.vermont.gov/websites/UVA/Reporting/StewardshipGuide.pdf)general landowner guide to stewardship and management optionsNamed in the county forester's resource list.
Vermont Land Survey Library (maps.vcgi.vermont.gov/landsurveylibrary)survey records and a measuring tool for exploring exclusion areasNamed by the county forester as a measuring tool. The vault has no statement on how accurate its measurements are.
