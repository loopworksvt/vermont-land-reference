Names

Butter-and-eggs (common toadflax; Go Botany says "butter-and-eggs toadflax"), Linaria vulgaris, in the family Plantaginaceae. Go Botany lists the synonym Linaria linaria.

Range and habitat

Not native: Go Botany answers "No" to native to North America and records the plant as present in Vermont. It lists human-disturbed habitats and meadows and fields and does not classify its wetland status. Minnesota Wildflowers gives origin as Europe and lists part shade or sun; fields and along roads.

How to identify (by season; look-alikes and the key difference)

  • Height: 15 to 90 cm.
  • Flowers: orange and yellow, 19 to 33 mm long, with two prominent lips, a tube-shaped base and one nectar spur 10 to 13 mm long.
  • Leaves: simple, alternate and stalkless with smooth edges and a pointed tip, 20 to 60 mm long and 1 to 15 mm wide.
  • Look-alike (Go Botany): Linaria spartea, named without a comparison.
