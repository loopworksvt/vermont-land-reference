Withdrawal or subdivision that leaves under 25 enrolled acres

tripwireT-VT-003

A summary, not legal advice. This is what your reference library records about the rule, not a ruling on your land. Who to ask: current use program office, county forester.

The minimum is 25 acres exclusive of the housesite. If withdrawing land or a subdivision takes enrolled land under that, the whole parcel loses eligibility. Adding land to an enrollment has its own deadlines (application by September 1, plan and maps by October 1), so a later fix usually costs at least one year at full property tax. Re-enrolling needs a new application, a new plan and a new map.

Ask first: on what date is the land treated as out of the program, and when will the tax notice and the town's valuation arrive?

What triggers it
Withdraw land, subdivide, sell: enrolled land (exclusive of the housesite) falls below 25 acres
What follows
the parcel is no longer eligible and leaves the program; land use change tax applies only to land that was developed, not merely to land that became ineligible
How to avoid it
plan the exit: ask the Current Use office for the exit date and the first tax year at full value; re-enroll with a new application, plan and map for a later year; or add eligible acreage first, before the application deadline
Set by
Department of Taxes, Current Use program; FPR county forester
Source
32 V.S.A. § 3752(9)(A); 32 V.S.A. § 3757(a)
